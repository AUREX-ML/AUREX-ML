## 👨‍💻 About Me

> *"I build systems that connect electrical infrastructure, software, IoT, and AI into practical solutions for Africa's future."*

I'm an **Electrical Infrastructural Developer, Engineer, and Systems Developer** based in **Machakos, Kenya**.

My work sits at the intersection of:
- ⚡ Electrical & Electronics Engineering
- 🌐 IoT and Connected Devices
- ☁️ Cloud Platforms and Monitoring
- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 🔋 Open-Source Energy Management Systems

I'm passionate about building **intelligent infrastructure for Africa** — making energy more visible, reliable, coordinated, and useful. I explore how software communicates with hardware, how data improves decision-making, and how open-source communities help young engineers grow into creators and founders.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🇰🇪 [Kenya Energy OS](https://github.com/AUREX-ML)
> *Digital Infrastructure for Energy Visibility*

A founder-level energy systems project improving visibility, monitoring, and coordination of distributed energy resources in Kenya. Connects software, IoT, open-source energy tools, and data systems to solve infrastructure challenges like operational blindness and last-mile visibility gaps.

`Energy Visibility` `DER` `Utility Digital Transformation` `IoT Monitoring` `OpenEMS`

---

### ⚡ [DERMS Concept](https://github.com/AUREX-ML)
> *Distributed Energy Resource Management System*

A system concept for helping utilities and energy stakeholders monitor, coordinate, and optimize distributed energy resources — solar PV, batteries, backup generators, and flexible loads.

`Grid Modernization` `DER Coordination` `Smart Infrastructure` `Energy Data Systems`

---

### 📊 [My Energy Management System](https://github.com/AUREX-ML)
> *Facility Energy Monitoring Prototype*

A practical energy management project using open-source tools — MyEMS, Raspberry Pi, GitHub workflows, and energy monitoring concepts to collect energy data, visualize performance, and improve operational awareness.

`MyEMS` `Raspberry Pi` `Energy Dashboards` `Open-Source`

---

### 🔌 [OpenEMS Learning Track](https://github.com/AUREX-ML)
> *Open-Source Energy Management Development*

A technical learning path for understanding OpenEMS as a platform for modern energy management, renewable integration, battery systems, and intelligent energy control.

`OpenEMS` `Solar PV` `Battery Systems` `Software-Hardware Integration`

---

### 🗂️ [Founder Operating System](https://github.com/AUREX-ML)
> *Personal System for Turning Ideas into Projects*

A personal productivity and founder-development system to organize ideas, research, projects, technical learning, GitHub work, and documentation into an actionable startup-building workflow.

`Founder Development` `Project Organization` `Technical Documentation`
